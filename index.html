Reading a Flux Bar

  • Thin wick: the full high–low range, same as a candle.
  • Body: the value area, the price zone where 70% of the bar's volume traded. It shows where the market accepted price, not just where it opened and closed.
  • Body colour: delta, i.e. aggressive buys minus aggressive sells. Green means buyers were lifting offers, red means sellers were hitting bids, grey means balanced. The more saturated, the more one-sided.
  • Gold tick: point of control, the busiest single price in the bar.
  • Left / right ticks: open and close, so nothing from a candle is lost.
  • ◆ diamonds: absorption. Aggressive flow went one way but price closed the other way, meaning a big passive player took the other side. Green below the bar means sellers were absorbed; red above means buyers were absorbed.

The clock

On the volume clock, every bar contains the same volume. Busy periods produce more bars, quiet periods produce fewer, so each bar carries a similar amount of information. Tempo shows how fast each bar filled.

Indicators

  • VWAP ±1σ/±2σ (blue): volume-weighted fair value for the session, with exact volume-weighted bands.
  • Profile (right): volume at price for the visible bars. Gold = POC, blue = value area. On the chart: solid gold lines are high-volume nodes (acceptance, often support or resistance); dotted grey lines are low-volume nodes (rejection, price moves through them fast).
  • CVD: cumulative delta. If price makes a new high and CVD doesn't, the move lacks aggressive buyers.
  • Tempo: log₂ of how much faster than usual the bar filled. Orange bars ≥ 1 mean at least twice normal speed: urgency.
  • Efficiency: net move ÷ distance travelled over 14 bars. Above 0.6 means trending, below 0.3 means ranging. It tells you which playbook to use.

Controls: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag or scroll the price axis to stretch it, and double-click to reset. The full trading playbook is in docs/TRADING_GUIDE.md.
